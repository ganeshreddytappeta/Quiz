Guidelines

Rules & Instructions

Official competition protocol for all participating teams in AIKYAM 2K26 | FUN&TECH.

  • 👥
    Team Structure: Registered teams from 2nd and 4th B.Tech CSIT-A compete collaboratively using a single dedicated portal device.
  • 🎯
    30 MCQ Challenges: The battle consists of exactly 30 questions (15 Non-Technical/Fun + 15 Technical). Each question has 4 choices (A, B, C, D).
  • ⭐
    Scoring Formula: Each correct answer awards +1 point. There is no negative marking; unsubmitted questions earn 0 points.
  • 🔒
    Decision Lock-In: Once an option is chosen and submitted, it is immediately locked in for that round. Confer with your team before confirming!
  • 👑
    Host Controlled Progression: There are no erratic countdown timers. The competition moves forward under host coordination to ensure an equitable playing field.
  • 🤝
    Fair Play & Ethics: External unauthorized lookups, multi-tab bypasses, or device disruptions result in disqualification. Maintain academic and competitive integrity.
